Corporate Boardroom & Executive Conference Suites

Custom 20-seater conference suite, dual-monitor video conference wall, acoustic timber panelling, and integrated wire management.

Serving as the centerpiece of a corporate regional headquarters, this project required high-tolerance craftsmanship. The 20-seater boardroom table was constructed with internal steel sub-frames and wrapped in bookmatched walnut veneer with motorized pop-up AV raceways.

‍

The suite features architectural acoustic panelling, dual video conferencing display integration, suspended acoustic wooden ceiling trays, and 3D geometric acoustic block wall installations delivered to strict schedule milestones.
