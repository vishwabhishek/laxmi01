## 3. Main Business Activities and Services

Laxmi Enterprises works in furniture, joinery and commercial interior
execution.

### Furniture and installation

-   Installation and assembly of factory-made furniture.
-   Bulk furniture installation for commercial projects.
-   Installation of furniture and components supplied by a client or
    main contractor.
-   Furniture execution as part of larger commercial interior projects.

### Carpentry, joinery and fabrication

-   On-site carpentry.
-   Custom joinery and cabinetry.
-   On-site fabrication using plywood, laminates and related
    accessories.
-   Fitting and installation of furniture components.
-   Joinery and furniture finishing work.

### Surface finishes and interior elements

-   Laminate and veneer work.
-   Finishing work associated with furniture and joinery.
-   Partitions and panelling.
-   Related interior fit-out execution.

### Broader execution work

-   Commercial interior fit-out execution.
-   Small turnkey renovation works, where suitable.
-   Labour-based execution and installation work within larger projects.

The business's strongest positioning is as a **specialist furniture,
carpentry and joinery execution contractor**, rather than as a
design-led interior studio.

## 4. How Projects and Contracts Typically Work

The user described several types of engagement:

1.  **Labour-only contracts:** The client or main contractor supplies
    the materials, while Laxmi Enterprises provides labour and
    execution.
2.  **Execution with client-supplied design and materials:** A main
    contractor provides the design and materials; Laxmi Enterprises
    handles the furniture or installation execution.
3.  **Installation contracts:** Factory-made furniture or components are
    supplied by another party and installed by Laxmi Enterprises.
4.  **On-site fabrication:** In some projects, the team carries out
    carpentry/fabrication on site using plywood, laminates, accessories
    and related materials.
5.  **Broader fit-out or renovation execution:** The business may take
    on smaller turnkey renovation or fit-out work when appropriate.

The business has often operated as an execution/subcontracting partner
within projects led by other contractors.
