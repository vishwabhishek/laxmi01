FAQs

What materials and plywood grades do you use for commercial projects?

We work strictly to client Bill of Quantities (BOQ) specifications, typically using BWP / BWR grade commercial plywood, HDHMR boards, and fire-retardant substrates from certified brands with premium laminates, veneers, or PU finishes.

What is your typical project kickoff lead time and delivery schedule?

We deploy verified carpentry crews within 48 to 72 hours of drawing sign-off and site clearance. Project timelines range from 2–4 weeks for fast-track office clusters to 2–6 months for multi-floor commercial fit-outs.

Can you execute projects on a labor-only or turnkey basis?

Yes. We provide flexible engagement models: both labor-only contracting (executing directly from client-supplied materials) and full item-rate / turnkey execution covering procurement, fabrication, and installation.

Do you deploy supervision on-site throughout the project lifecycle?

Yes. Our Founder & Principal Supervisor personally directs on-site operations daily, ensuring strict compliance with architectural shop drawings, quality checklists, and safety standards until joint snag handover.
