FAQs

Which materials do you use?

We build to your BOQ. Typical materials: BWP/BWR plywood, HDHMR and fire-retardant boards from certified brands, finished in laminate, veneer or PU.

How quickly can you start, and how long do projects take?

Crews are on site within 48–72 hours of drawing sign-off and site clearance. Typical duration: 2–4 weeks for office clusters, 2–6 months for multi-floor fit-outs.

Do you offer labour-only contracts?

Yes. We offer labour-only (client-supplied material) or full turnkey/item-rate contracts covering procurement, fabrication and installation.

Is the work supervised on site?

Yes. Dedicated trade supervisors oversee site operations daily against shop drawings, quality checklists and safety standards. In critical conditions, our senior specialists are also directly involved through to joint snag handover.

What is included in Laxmi Enterprises' turnkey interior contracting?

Turnkey interior contracting by Laxmi Enterprises provides single-source accountability across civil demolition, space reconfiguration, drywall partitions, architectural joinery, electrical conduits, plumbing lines, and surface finishes. We coordinate all specialized MEP trades under veteran site supervision to ensure seamless alignment with approved architect drawings and client schedules.

How quickly can turnkey interior projects mobilize and complete?

Turnkey interior mobilization begins within forty-eight to seventy-two hours of drawing sign-off and site handover. Typical commercial office fit-outs of five to twenty thousand square feet require four to eight weeks, while large multi-floor corporate headquarters are delivered within three to six months following rigorous milestone schedules.

Key Project Delivery Principles & Trade Disciplines

  • Single-Source Responsibility: Seamless coordination across civil, electrical, plumbing, and joinery.
  • Rapid On-Site Mobilization: Crews mobilized within 48–72 hours of drawing approvals and site handover.
  • Stringent Drawing Adherence: Laser measurements eliminating site errors and material wastage.
